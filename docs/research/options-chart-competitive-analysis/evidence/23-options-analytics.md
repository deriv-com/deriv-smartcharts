## Tool profiles

### 1. OptionStrat (strategy visualizer, Optimizer, Flow)

**What it shows and how it is drawn**
- **P/L table (heatmap), the default view** (S1, verified). The dark-navy page shows a grid:
  - **Rows:** underlying prices. The left column shows the price ($798, $795, ...) with % distance from spot in small grey text.
  - **Columns:** future dates, grouped by month header (Oct, Nov), with weekday letters.
  - **Cells:** P/L in $. Profit cells are green and loss cells are red; saturation scales with magnitude.
  - A dashed horizontal separator marks the current-price row.
  - A segmented control switches the cell metric: Profit/Loss $, Profit/Loss %, Contract Value, % of Max Risk, More.
- **P/L graph** (S1, verified, after clicking Graph):
  - **Axes:** x is underlying price, y is P/L $.
  - **Lines:** the expiration P/L line is a red-to-green gradient line with the area to the zero line filled (red below, green above). A **dotted vertical line marks the strike**, a **solid blue vertical line marks the breakeven**, and a white horizontal line marks $0.
  - **Date slider:** dragging the slider under the chart to an earlier date ("Mon Nov 2nd (6d)") adds curves to a legend, each in its own style:
    - a solid curve for the selected date
    - a dashed curve for an intermediate date (Fri Nov 20th, 24d)
    - a dotted curve for expiration (32d)
  - The right side shows "(26d remaining)". A spot marker label ($768.13) sits above the chart.
- **Under-chart controls** (S1, verified):
  - RANGE slider (±4.3%)
  - IMPLIED VOLATILITY slider (14.4%, with ×1, ×2, ×3 tick marks and a reset icon)
  - Table/Graph toggle
- **Header stats and timeline** (S1, verified):
  - Header stats: Net debit, Max loss, Max profit, **Chance of profit** (padlocked on free), Breakeven ("Above $779.70 (+1.9%)").
  - Greeks row: Δ, Θ, Γ, ν, ρ.
  - Expiration picker: a horizontal timeline of date chips grouped by month.
  - Strike picker: a horizontal ruler with a draggable green "765C" chip.
- **Chance of profit:** "based on the implied move" per the tutorial (S4, claimed). It uses the chain's implied volatility (S8, claimed).
- **Optimizer** (S3, verified):
  - Six round sentiment buttons: Very Bearish (red down arrow), Bearish, Neutral, Directional (purple split arrow), Bullish (filled green), Very Bullish.
  - Inputs: target price, optional budget, and an expiry chip timeline.
  - A **"Max Return ↔ Max Chance" slider**.
  - Output: a grid of strategy cards (Long Call, Covered Call, Cash-Secured Put, ...). Each card has a mini payoff chart (red/green gradient fill, blue spot line), Return-on-risk %, Chance % (locked) and "Open in Builder".
- **Flow** (S2, verified):
  - Two-column Summary: "Bullish Flow" with green horizontal bars ranked by premium, against "Bearish Flow" in red.
  - Tabs: Live / Historical / News / Congress / Insider.
  - A half-circle sentiment gauge.
  - A Filters & Alerts side panel: buy/sell side, single/split/sweep/block, calls/puts/spreads, Volume > OI, above ask/below bid, "Chance was …" filter.
  - The page states data is delayed 15 minutes on free.
- **Paid extras** (S6, claimed): net Greeks over time, per-expiration IV, a volume overlay of most-active strikes, alerts on Flow.

**Standout:** The P/L-over-time table and a date slider that overlays several dated P/L curves (verified). Together they make time decay visible and teachable.

**Praise:**
- Modern and easy to use (S5, claimed).
- Users say it teaches strategy mechanics (S7 reviews, claimed).
- The payoff diagram and sliders are "genuinely useful" for multi-leg learning (S8, claimed).

**Complaints:**
- Paywall creep; mobile crashes reported on Reddit (S8, claimed).
- IV is held constant, and earnings/dividends are not modelled (S6, claimed).
- No per-leg IV, uniform IV shift (S8, claimed).
- Flow "shows activity, not intent" (S8, claimed).
- English only (S7 review, claimed).

**Data needs:** full option chain (bid/ask/mid), IV per strike/expiry, a pricing model (S6 says American model; claimed), Greeks, and OPRA trade tape for Flow.

### 2. Market Chameleon (IV rank, implied moves, earnings moves, trade ideas)

**What it shows and how it is drawn**
- **Header strip** (S9 and S10, verified): 30-Day IV with change, and **"IV30 % Rank: 52% Moderate"** (a word label next to the number).
- **Intraday IV chart** (S9, verified): a dark-green IV line over blue/orange volume bars.
- **IV vs HV page** (S10, verified):
  - Plain-English narrative: a percentile-rank paragraph and an "IV vs 20-day HV" paragraph.
  - A Summary IV vs HV table (IV30, 20D HV, 252D HV: current / 52-wk avg / high / low).
  - Tabs "Historical IV -vs- Realized" and "IV Term Structure".
  - The full chart has a 1y/3y/5y/Max range selector and sits behind a "Register Free / 7-Day Free Trial" overlay.
- **Implied Moves page** (S11, verified):
  - **Chart:** one column per day for the last 20 days. A **blue error bar** shows the ±market-implied one-day move derived from the nearest-expiry ATM IV. A **green or red bar** shows the actual move, with grey bars for days not yet loaded.
  - **Table:** Date | Last Price | Implied Move (±1.0%) | Actual Move (+0.7% green / −0.5% red) | **Within Range? Yes/No**, with a summary of days inside and outside the range (premium-gated).
- **Earnings charts** (S12, claimed): expected vs actual earnings move and IV before and after earnings for 12 quarters, plus average "volatility crush".
- **Trade-idea cards** (S9, verified): dark cards per strategy, e.g. "Long Call: Best By Theoretical Edge", "Credit Iron Condor: Best by Theoretical Edge + 70% Win Rate". Each card shows:
  - Market Price vs Theoretical Value, with an "Edge" %
  - a ring/donut **"Hist. Win Rate"** (30%, 74%)

**Standout:** "Implied vs actual move, inside or outside range" history (verified). It is a simple calibration visual that shows whether the expected move held.

**Praise:** Deep history (options data since 2014), earnings backtesting called best-in-class, IV rank/percentile and term structure (S13, claimed).

**Complaints:** Clunky, dense interface; no mobile app; 15-minute delay; confusing tiers (S13, claimed). Many charts need login (observed on S12, verified).

**Data needs:** ATM IV by expiry, historical IV/HV series, earnings calendar, end-of-day and historical option mid prices for backtests.

### 3. Option Alpha (automation bots, payoff diagrams, 0DTE Oracle)

**What it shows and how it is drawn**
- **Payoff diagram** (S14, verified screenshot; light theme):
  - Iron-condor-shaped expiration P/L with a **mint-green fill above zero** and a **pink fill below**.
  - Dashed breakeven verticals labelled with prices (224.21, 238.29), a dashed spot line with a blue price tag (231.58), and strike ticks on the x-axis.
  - The stats grid above it: credit, mid, DTE, breakeven, max profit/loss, **Reward/Risk, Alpha, EV, Prob of Profit (77%), Prob of Max Profit (48%), Prob of Max Loss (14%)**.
  - The page says probabilities "update in real-time" with live pricing.
- **0DTE Oracle** (S15, verified): it ranks 0DTE and next-day trades by **backtest on 1 year of intraday minute data** rather than by model probability. Its stated reason is that Black-Scholes probabilities are less accurate for 0DTE because the time component is too short. It refreshes every minute.
- **0DTE backtester and Touch exit** (S16, verified):
  - The backtester shows exit reasons and a monthly P/L breakdown.
  - A new **"Touch" exit** closes a position when price comes within a $ or % distance of the strike.
- **Bots** (S17, verified):
  - Automations run every minute, on a schedule, or on a button.
  - Scanner automations open positions and monitor automations exit them.
  - Logs: bot log, automation log, decision log (with the data used), position log, P/L dashboard.
  - Bot entries can filter on probability of profit, e.g. POP > 70% (S14 FAQ, verified).

**Standout:** Probability-of-profit plus probability-of-max-profit/loss on every position, and an explicit "backtest instead of probability" approach for 0DTE.

**Praise:** Community-shared bot templates; free through partner brokers; extra entry filters (S18, claimed).

**Complaints:** Few indicators (MACD/RSI); bot templates "50/50"; backtests limited; not for beginners (S18, claimed; only about 10 reviews).

**Data needs:** chain, Greeks/IV, live quotes, minute-level history for backtests, broker connectivity.

### 4. SpotGamma (key gamma levels, HIRO, TRACE heatmap)

**What it shows and how it is drawn**
- **TRACE** (S19, verified, official guide). The page has two panes.
  - **Strike plot (left):** horizontal bars per SPX strike with three lenses:
    - GEX: **blue = positive gamma, red = negative gamma**
    - OI: **calls orange, puts blue**
    - Net OI
    - Extras: a 0DTE-only toggle, and tooltips showing percentile and daily min/max. **Historical dots** (white, yellow, blue) mark the position 10, 30 and 60 minutes earlier.
  - **Heatmap (right):**
    - **Axes:** the x-axis is time of day; the left y-axis is SPX strikes; the right y-axis is the HIRO signal.
    - **Overlays:** candles at 1, 5 or 15 minutes.
    - **Cell colours:** the colour encodes notional gamma or delta. Under the Gamma lens, **blue zones = lower expected volatility / pinning, red = higher volatility**, and white (light mode) or black (dark mode) = transition.
    - **Lines:** **contour lines** show where the colour changes fast, and a **purple line = HIRO flow** is overlaid.
    - **Models:** a model dropdown switches Gamma / Delta Pressure / Charm Pressure. A participant dropdown switches market makers, customers, pro customers, firms and broker-dealers, with an invert toggle.
    - **Gauge:** a **Stability Gauge** gives the likelihood of a large move in the next 10 minutes.
  - **Navigation:**
    - a Key Levels toggle
    - zoom slider and drag-zoom
    - a calendar to view history or **5-day forward projections**
    - a **Timeline Slider that replays positioning minute by minute**
    - hover tooltips with time, SPX level and Greek value
    - light/dark mode and custom colours
  - Updates every minute from 09:00 to 16:30 ET, and every 10 minutes overnight.
- **HIRO** (S20, verified, official guide):
  - **Lines:** HIRO is drawn as a **blue candlestick or line** for cumulative hedging-pressure delta notional on the right axis. **Price is a white line** on the left axis.
  - **Put/Call view:** puts dark blue, calls orange.
  - **Next Expiry view (0DTE / next Friday):** adds a **green** line.
  - **Settings:** rolling window, intervals from 5 s to 30 min, line/candle/OHLC.
  - **Indicators:** technical indicators can be applied to price *or* to HIRO.
  - **Flow Alerts:** drawn as **vertical lines on the chart at the time an alert fired**, plus a red bell.
  - **Key-level overlays:** Call Wall, Put Wall, Hedge Wall, Key Gamma Strike.
  - **Level alerts:** for "breached" or "within 1%" of a Call/Put Wall.
- **Key levels** (S21, claimed): Call Wall, Put Wall, Zero Gamma, Volatility Trigger and implied move, published daily. They are also available as an indicator on TrendSpider.
- **Published hold-rate statistics** (S21, claimed): the Call Wall held in 83% of sessions and the Put Wall in 89% (May 2019 to May 2024).

**Standout:** The time × price heatmap with a replay slider, and alerts drawn as vertical event lines on the chart.

**Praise:** Among the most advanced market-maker positioning tools; useful Founder's Notes; long track record for levels (S22, claimed). Users quoted in the guide say they leave TRACE open all day (S19, vendor-selected testimonial).

**Complaints:** Expensive; analyses accused of hindsight bias; HIRO changes too fast to use live; incoherent UI; refund issues; no public API (S22, claimed; partly competitor sources). Sources disagree on which plan tiers include HIRO and TRACE.

**Data needs:** full OPRA tape with trade side, open interest, a proprietary dealer-positioning model, and Greeks across all strikes and expiries, recomputed every minute.

### 6. Added: Barchart (expected move and P/L chart)

- **Expected-move chart** (S27, verified):
  - Six months of the price line (blue), with **"E" earnings flags** on past earnings.
  - At "today" a light-blue vertical band. From there, **green dashed (upper) and red dashed (lower) lines step forward** to the next weekly and monthly expiries, forming a widening cone.
  - Horizontal dashed green/red lines extend the current range back across history for reference.
  - Below the chart: the actual move after each of the last 4 earnings (green/red %) and the average move.
  - The method is the ATM straddle (85%) or a 1-σ IV calculation (S27 snippet, claimed).
- **P/L chart tool:** profit, loss, breakevens, probability of profit, plus an Expected Move tab and a Volatility tab (S28, claimed).

### Others considered and dropped (with reason)

- **Quiver Quantitative:** alternative-data aggregator (congress, lobbying, WSB). It has no dedicated options-flow or options-visual tooling (S37, claimed), so it is not relevant to chart-level options analytics.
- **Tradytics:** flow, AI signals ("Bullseye") and dashboards that overlap Unusual Whales' flow and GEX (S37, claimed). It adds no distinct chart visual beyond AI scores, and the Deriv product has no option tape to feed it. Dropped from the matrix.
- **thinkorswim:** noted only as a reference. Its Analyze tab has a probability-cone overlay and right-click probability ITM/OTM/touch (S38, claimed). Brokers are presumably covered in other research files.

## Adaptable visual analytics for Deriv

### Why the maths is easy for Deriv

**Volatility is known in advance.** Deriv publishes that its volatility indices have **constant volatility** from 10% to 250% (S35, verified). The exact levels are 10, 15, 25, 30, 50, 75, 90, 100, 150 and 250 (S36, claimed). Ticks arrive every 2 s, or every 1 s for the "(1s)" indices (S35, verified). That removes the hardest input in options analytics, implied volatility: σ is a constant, so every probability below can be computed **client-side in SmartCharts** without an option chain.

**Model and notation.** Treat the index as geometric Brownian motion with zero price drift, which gives log-drift ν = −σ²/2.

**Assumptions to confirm with Deriv's quants:**
- the exact driftless and annualisation convention (these indices run 24/7, so I assume T in years = seconds / (365·86 400))
- that the published σ is annualised

Definitions:
- T = remaining time in years. For tick contracts, T = n_ticks × tick_interval / 31 536 000.
- s = σ√T, the 1-σ log-move over the remaining time.
- k = ln(B/S), the log distance from spot S to barrier B.

Worked scale for **V75 (σ = 0.75)**: 1σ ≈ 0.042% over 5 ticks of 2 s; 0.10% over 1 min; 0.23% over 5 min; 0.80% over 1 h.

Core formulas:
- **P(finish above B)** = N((−k + νT)/s). This is Higher/Lower, Rise/Fall (B = entry spot) and the CALL ITM probability for Vanillas.
- **P(touch B before T)**, one barrier, continuous monitoring:
  P = N((−|k| + ν'T)/s) + e^{2ν'|k|/σ²}·N((−|k| − ν'T)/s),
  where ν' = ν for an upper barrier and −ν for a lower one. For tick-length horizons drift is negligible and P ≈ 2·N(−|k|/s) (reflection principle). OptionsBench states the same rule of thumb, that touch ≈ 2× finish beyond (S38, claimed).
  Deriv settles on discrete ticks, so apply the **Broadie–Glasserman–Kou correction**: shift the barrier outward by e^{±0.5826·σ√Δt}, with Δt the tick interval. Without it, touch probability is overstated.
- **P(end between L and U)** = N(d_U) − N(d_L) style difference. **P(stay between)**, the double no-touch, uses the standard series (Kunitomo–Ikeda; about 10 terms converge for short T). A small Monte Carlo is the fallback.
- **Accumulators.** The barrier re-centres on each previous spot, so per-tick survival p ≈ 2·N(b/(σ√Δt)) − 1 for barrier half-width b (log). Survival after n ticks is pⁿ, and expected ticks before knock-out is p/(1−p). Independence follows from the re-centring.
- **Multipliers (no expiry).** In driftless log space, P(take-profit hit before stop-out) ≈ d_SL/(d_TP + d_SL) (gambler's ruin), with distances measured in log price. P(stop-out within t) is the touch formula.
- **Digits** (last digit of the quote, assuming the RNG is uniform):
  - each digit 10%
  - Over d = (9−d)/10
  - Under d = d/10
  - Even/Odd 50%
  - Matches 10%, Differs 90%
  These are combinatorial, with no σ needed.

**Responsible-trading and commercial caveat (product decision, not research):**
- A model probability shown next to a Deriv payout reveals the house margin, because price/payout implies a probability.
- It may also be read as advice.
- Option Alpha's 0DTE Oracle explicitly distrusts short-horizon model probabilities (S15, verified). For Deriv's synthetics the model *is* the generator, so this objection is weaker, but legal and compliance should own the decision on which numbers to show.

### Ideas (each with its source inspiration)

**1. Expected-move cone to expiry on the price chart** (from Barchart S27 and OptionCharts S29, verified)
- **Fits:** Rise/Fall, Higher/Lower, Touch/No Touch, Ends/Stays Between, Vanillas, Turbos, and pre-trade on all volatility indices.
- **Maths:** S·e^{±z·σ√t} for t from 0 to expiry, with z = 1 and 2.
- **UI sketch:**
  - The cone starts at the current tick and opens rightward to the expiry marker on the time axis (3.5).
  - Fills: a light translucent fill for 1σ (≈68%) and a lighter fill for 2σ (≈95%). Upper and lower edges are dotted, green above and red below, as in OptionCharts.
  - The cone re-anchors on every tick and narrows as expiry approaches.
  - It is fully native for tick durations (5 ticks → a tiny cone, 1 h → a wide one).
  - It is a toggle in the chart settings and off by default.
- **Implementation:** a Flutter-side painter fed by σ, tick interval and expiry epoch. The Deriv chart already draws barrier shades (`src/components/Shade.tsx`, `src/store/BarrierStore.ts`).

**2. Barrier probability readout and hover probability split** (from TradingView's "← 4.1% · 95.9% →" tooltip, S32 verified, and OptionStrat's chance of profit, S1)
- **Fits:** Higher/Lower, Touch/No Touch, Turbos (knock-out), Multipliers (stop-out / take-profit levels).
- **Maths:** the finish and touch formulas above, with the BGK correction.
- **UI sketch:**
  - On the barrier line's right-edge tag, add a second chip, for example "Touch ≈ 23%" or "Ends above ≈ 31%".
  - While the user drags the barrier (4.1), the chip updates every frame (4.5). It is cheap: one normal-CDF call.
  - With the crosshair hovering a price level, the tooltip shows "▼ 69% · 31% ▲" (probability of finishing below/above) for the selected expiry.
  - For Turbos, the knock-out line carries "Knock-out before expiry ≈ x%".
  - For Multipliers, the stop-out and take-profit lines carry "TP first ≈ x% / SL first ≈ y%".

**3. Probability heat-shading ("fan chart") instead of a single cone** (from SpotGamma TRACE's time × price heatmap, S19 verified, and the OptionCharts PDF, S30 verified)
- **Fits:** Touch/No Touch, Stays Between, Accumulators.
- **Maths:** the touch or finish probability evaluated on a grid of (time-to-expiry, price level). This is cheap in a shader or a 50×50 grid per frame.
- **UI sketch:**
  - Behind the future part of the chart (right of the last tick), shade the time × price region by probability.
  - Use a TRACE-like diverging scale: blue = "likely to be reached / stay inside", red = "unlikely". Draw contour lines at 25/50/75%.
  - In TRACE these are called contours (S19, verified).
  - Add a legend scale on the right.
  - For Stays Between, overlay the two barriers. The shaded interior then fades as time extends, which shows visually why longer durations are harder.

**4. Live win-probability strip for open contracts** (inspired by Option Alpha's real-time POP and probability of max profit/loss, S14 verified, and TRACE's timeline replay, S19 verified)
- **Fits:** every open contract with a binary outcome (Rise/Fall, Higher/Lower, Touch/No Touch, Between), and Turbos (survival).
- **Maths:** recompute P(win | current spot, remaining ticks or time) on each tick.
- **UI sketch:**
  - A thin 24-px band under the main chart (like a sports win-probability graph) plots P(win) from contract start to now, with a 50% midline.
  - The band is green above 50% and red below.
  - At settlement it snaps to 0 or 100% and feeds the existing outcome animation (3.6).
  - In contract replay (7.1) it scrubs with the timeline.
  - For Accumulators, the strip shows survival probability for the next N ticks.

**5. Accumulator survival curve and payout-growth vs survival** (inspired by the "% of Max Risk" and Profit/Loss views, OptionStrat S1, and Option Alpha's EV stat, S14)
- **Fits:** Accumulators.
- **Data:** growth rate g, barrier width b and σ. The current Accumulators band and growth-rate ladder already exist (`src/types/props.types.ts:346-351`, `:480-515`).
- **UI sketch:**
  - When the growth-rate band is pressed, a small popover chart appears. Its x-axis is ticks held (0 to N); its y-axis shows two curves:
    - payout multiple (1+g)ⁿ, rising, green
    - survival pⁿ, falling, red
  - It is labelled with expected ticks before knock-out.
  - Moving between growth-rate rungs morphs the curves. This is an education piece that makes the risk/reward trade-off concrete.
  - Compliance should review whether to show the product of the two curves (EV).

**6. Expected vs actual "stayed inside?" history** (from Market Chameleon Implied Moves with its "Within Range? Yes/No" column, S11 verified; Barchart's prior earnings moves, S27; SpotGamma's published level hold-rates, S21)
- **Fits:** Stays Between / Goes Outside, Ends Between, Accumulators, Touch/No Touch.
- **Data:** recent tick history only.
- **UI sketch:**
  - Under the trade panel or in a chart drawer, a strip of the last 20 windows of the selected duration (for example 20 × 5-minute windows).
  - Each window shows a blue error bar (±1σ expected) and a green or red bar (actual close-to-close move), plus "16/20 inside ±1σ".
  - For constant-volatility indices this should hover near 68%. The point is to calibrate intuition and counter the gambler's fallacy, not to predict.
  - Label it clearly as historical.

**7. Payoff mini-diagram cards in the contract-type picker** (from the TradingView gallery cards with mini payoff and sentiment tag, S32 verified; OptionStrat Optimizer cards, S3 verified)
- **Fits:** all contract types, mainly for onboarding (11.5).
- **UI sketch:**
  - Each contract type gets a 120×70 thumbnail payoff (x = price at expiry relative to entry, y = P/L): a binary step for Rise/Fall, a hockey-stick for Vanilla, a ramp with knock-out cut for Turbo, a linear-with-stop-out shape for Multiplier, a staircase growth for Accumulator.
  - Thumbnails use green/red fills, a plain-English one-liner and an outlook tag (Up / Down / Range / Breakout).
  - Optional: an OptionStrat-style outlook selector (6 round icons) that filters the cards, with a "Higher payout ↔ Higher chance" slider that moves the barrier offset. This maps directly onto OptionStrat's "Max Return ↔ Max Chance" (S3, verified).

**8. P/L-over-time table and time slider for Vanillas and Turbos** (from the OptionStrat table and date slider, S1 verified, and TradingView what-if, S34 verified)
- **Fits:** Vanillas (time value matters) and Turbos.
- **Maths:** Black-Scholes with the known σ, r = 0 (to confirm), and Deriv's contract size.
- **UI sketch:**
  - A side panel toggles Graph / Table.
  - **Graph:** the expiry payoff (solid) and the "now" curve (dashed). A time slider adds dotted curves for "+N minutes", using OptionStrat's solid/dashed/dotted legend.
  - **Table:** rows are price levels as ±% from spot; columns are time checkpoints to expiry; cells are green/red by P/L (verified pattern in S1).
  - Tap a cell to place a horizontal guide on the main chart at that price.

**9. Volatility regime chip for each synthetic index** (from Market Chameleon "IV30 % Rank 52% Moderate", S10 verified, and Unusual Whales "Regime: Dampening", S23 verified)
- **Fits:** all contracts (6.5).
- **Maths:** for volatility indices, the published σ is constant, so the chip shows "σ 75% · 1-min 1σ ≈ ±0.10%". The ± figure converts σ into a tangible price move at the current spot.
- **For Crash/Boom, Jump, Step and Range Break:** show *realised* volatility vs long-run, as a percentile with a word label (Calm / Normal / Active).
- **For real-market symbols (forex, commodities):** use realised volatility (EWMA), labelled "estimate".

**10. Proximity and probability alerts drawn as vertical event lines** (from SpotGamma HIRO "Within 1% of Call Wall" alerts and alert vertical lines, S20 verified; Option Alpha Touch exit, S16 verified)
- **Fits:** open Touch/No Touch, Turbos, Multipliers, Accumulators.
- **UI sketch:** the host app can subscribe to rules such as:
  - "price within X of barrier"
  - "touch probability > Y%"
  - "accumulator band breached in the last N ticks"
  When a rule fires, the chart draws a thin vertical line at that tick with an icon (8.1), and the line persists in history (7.2).

**11. "How would this setup have done?" historical win-rate donut** (from the Market Chameleon "Hist. Win Rate" ring, S9 verified, and Option Alpha 0DTE Oracle backtests, S15 verified)
- **Fits:** Rise/Fall, Higher/Lower, Touch, Digits, Accumulators, all on synthetic history.
- **Data:** replay of the last 24 h of ticks, buying the identical contract every N ticks.
- **UI sketch:** a small ring next to the payout showing "Won 47% of 1,200 simulated entries in the last 24 h", with the theoretical figure beside it.
- **Strong compliance caveat:** past performance; may encourage trading. Possibly an education mode only.

**Not adaptable (and why):**
- GEX/HIRO/TRACE dealer-positioning and options-flow feeds (SG, UW, OS Flow) need an exchange option tape and open interest. Synthetic indices have neither.
- The closest analogue is **Deriv-internal aggregate positioning**: where other clients' barriers or strikes cluster, shown as a TRACE strike-plot-like histogram on the price axis (6.1/6.2). This is commercially and legally sensitive (it reveals the book), so it needs a product decision.

## Limitations

- **Login walls and blocks:**
  - OptionStrat's tutorial and features pages returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch. Their content is from search snippets (claimed). The live builder, Flow and Optimizer pages were inspected directly (verified).
  - Market Chameleon earnings charts and full IV history are login- or paywall-gated; only their descriptions were seen.
  - Unusual Whales Periscope and premium Greek views were not opened.
  - Chance of profit (OptionStrat), implied probability (OptionCharts) and the TRACE and HIRO products themselves are paid. TRACE and HIRO were documented from official PDF guides, not observed live.
- **Review reliability:**
  - Praise and complaints come mainly from third-party review sites, some of them competitors (FlashAlpha, TradeEcho, Options Matrix Pro), and from vendor-curated "Reddit reviews" pages.
  - I could not open actual Reddit threads in this session.
  - Option Alpha has only about 10 Trustpilot reviews.
- **Session scope:** All pages were viewed on 2026-10-27 at a single desktop viewport (800×600 screenshots). Mobile apps were not tested. Data was delayed or stale for non-subscribers, so live-update behaviour was not verified.
- **Deriv maths:**
  - The formulas assume GBM with the published constant σ, zero price drift and calendar-second annualisation. These need confirmation against Deriv's actual index-generation and pricing conventions.
  - They do not apply as-is to Crash/Boom, Jump, Step, Range Break or real-market symbols.
  - Digit probabilities assume a uniform last digit.
- **Gaps:**
  - Barchart cells marked "?" were not inspected beyond the expected-move page.
  - TradingView's builder was inspected for ES futures only.
  - Pricing for SpotGamma tiers is inconsistent across sources and was not resolved.
