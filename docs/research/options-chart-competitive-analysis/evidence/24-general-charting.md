## 1. Profiles

### 1.1 TradingView (web / desktop / mobile app)

- **Chart types:** 21 listed incl. Volume footprint, TPO, Renko, Kagi, Hollow candles [TV1, V].
- **Intervals:** custom intervals incl. seconds and range bars [TV1, V]; second- and tick-based intervals are paid-plan features [TV2, V]. Tick intervals are fixed at **1T / 10T / 100T / 1000T**, not customisable; not supported with Renko/Kagi/P&F/Line break, Bar Replay or deep backtesting; at expansion launch web/desktop only (not mobile) [TV5, O].
- **Indicators:** 400+ built-in indicators/strategies + 100,000+ public (Pine) scripts [TV1, V]. Per-chart indicator caps by plan: 2 / 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 [TV2, V].
- **Drawing tools:** 110+ "smart drawing tools" [TV1, V]. Includes Long/Short Position tool that can be converted into a limit order with SL/TP prefilled [third-party guides via search, C].
- **Layouts:** up to 16 charts per screen with symbol/timeframe/drawing sync [TV1, V]; per plan 1/2/4/8/16 [TV2, V].
- **Replay:** Bar Replay, 9 speeds, autoplay, step mode, synchronized multi-chart replay [TV1, V]; brackets usable in Replay trading since Nov 2023 [TV4, O].
- **Alerts:** 13 built-in price alert conditions, multi-condition alerts (up to 5), watchlist alerts [TV1, V]; active-alert caps per plan 3 → 1,000 [TV2, V].
- **Trade from chart:** "Full journey chart trading" incl. on-chart order creation and drag-to-modify [TV1, V]. TP/SL bracket buttons are pulled out of an order/position line to a price; Shift+drag moves order with brackets [TV3, V]. Chart settings toggle positions, P&L, brackets P&L, executions [third-party, C].
- **Options:** Options chain (real-time quotes, IV, Greeks), Strategy Builder with P&L + Greeks profile charts and what-if (time/IV shift), volatility curves per expiration/ATM vol [TV1, V; TV6, O]. Expiration P&L vs T+0 line [TV6, O]. Third-party says builder shows max profit/loss and "win rate"; no native probability cone found (only community Pine scripts) [TV7, C]. Single-leg options orders from chart via TradeStation/Alpaca/Tradier [TV7, C].
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** extensive (Alt+H horizontal line, Alt+R reset, Alt+T trend line, Alt+F Fib…) with an in-app shortcut dialog (Ctrl+/) [TV8, O; third-party, C].
- **Platforms:** web, desktop, mobile apps with synced layouts/watchlists/settings [TV1, V].
- **Standout:** breadth (400+ indicators, 110+ drawings, Pine), social scripts, options builder integrated with chart, brackets dragged straight out of the position line.
- **Praise:** the reference UX that everyone else is compared against (see MT5/cTrader comparisons [MT9, C]).
- **Complaints:** subscription management/billing surprises, weak support, data lag claims, a 2026 bug where saved candle charts loaded as line [TV9, C]. Tick charts gated to top plans and absent on mobile at launch [TV5, O].

### 1.2 TradingView Advanced Charts / Trading Platform (embeddable libraries)

- **Advanced Charts:** 100+ pre-built indicators; drawing tools stated as both 80+ and 110+ on the same page; up to 8 price scales; 670 KB; proprietary [TVL2, V].
- **Trading Platform** (superset of Advanced Charts): "Orders, positions, potential income and loss are displayed on the chart" via the Broker API; Advanced Order Ticket (trailing stop, SL, brackets); Account Manager; Depth of Market widget; up to 8 synchronized charts; Buy/Sell buttons + bid/ask lines; extra chart types Renko, P&F, Line Break, Kagi; seconds bars built from ticks; 900 KB; 19 chart types [TVL1, V; TVL2, V].
- **Trading primitives:** `createOrderLine` (onMove / onModify / onCancel, setQuantity, setText), `createPositionLine` (onClose / onModify / onReverse), `createExecutionShape`. Since v29 these were **removed from Advanced Charts and are Trading-Platform-only**, and became Promise-returning; v32.2 added setVisible/getVisible [TVL3, O].
- **Accessibility:** working toward WCAG 2.2; keyboard toolbar navigation (Alt+Z, or Tab with `accessible_keyboard_shortcuts`); colours WCAG 2.2 AA; screen readers VoiceOver, Narrator, JAWS, NVDA [TVL4, O].
- **Localization:** ~21 locales incl. `ar` and `he_IL`; RTL applies to toolbars/dialogs, **not the chart itself**; no custom locales; language switch requires re-creating the widget [TVL5, O].
- **Shortcuts:** shortcut dialog from tradingview.com is not included in the libraries [TV8, O].
- **Data:** no market data included [TVL6, V].
- **Standout:** the de-facto "ChartIQ alternative" for brokers; the order/position-line primitive API (drag, modify, cancel, close, reverse with tooltips) is the industry template for trade-from-chart.
- **Complaints / limits:** trading primitives moved behind the higher (Trading Platform) licence; RTL not applied inside the chart canvas [TVL3, TVL5, O].

### 1.3 TradingView Lightweight Charts (open source)

- Apache-2.0, requires TradingView attribution (attributionLogo option); ~17.7k GitHub stars; "one of the smallest and fastest financial HTML5 charts" [LWC1, V]; 35 KB [TVL2, V] (45 KB on another TradingView page [TVL6, V]).
- **No built-in indicators** — "you must implement them yourself" [TVL6, V]; maintainers in 2020 said indicators and drawing tools were not planned in core [LWC5, O].
- **Chart kinds:** time chart, Yield Curve chart, **Options chart** (numeric price on x-axis, aimed at option chains/price distributions), custom horizontal scale [LWC3, V].
- **5.x evolution:** multi-pane (5.0, 5.0.8), pane primitives, watermarks as plugins, series markers, MagnetOHLC crosshair (5.0.4), data conflation for large datasets (5.1.0), series hit-testing (5.2.0), Accessibility plugin (5.2.1) [LWC2, V].
- **Plugin examples** (official): Trend Line, Rectangle Drawing Tool, Vertical Line, User Price Alerts (via crosshair menu), Expiring Price Alerts, Session Highlighting, Volume Profile, Bands Indicator, Tooltip, Delta Tooltip, Heatmap/Stacked/Lollipop/Rounded-candle custom series, a11y [LWC4, V].
- **Standout:** "Expiring Price Alerts" and "Options chart" are directly relevant to short-duration options UIs; tiny footprint.
- **Complaints:** users must build indicators, drawing tools, alerts and trading UI themselves [LWC5, O].

### 1.4 MetaTrader 5

- **Counts:** 21 timeframes, 38 technical indicators, 44 analytical objects [MT1, V]. Chart types: bars, candles, line only [MT3, V]. Up to 100 open charts [MT3, V].
- **Tick data:** DOM includes a tick chart of every exchange trade (bubble size = volume) plus Time & Sales, for exchange instruments [MT4, V]. No native seconds/tick timeframes in the 21 list [MT1, MT3, V — absence inferred from list].
- **Trade from chart:** One-click trading panel; drag a position line to set SL/TP (platform asks SL vs TP by direction); pending orders from chart context menu; while dragging a level, tooltip shows potential **profit/loss in deposit currency and pips** [MT2, V]. In DOM: drag limit orders (SL/TP move with it), drag across bid/ask flips Limit↔Stop [MT4, V].
- **Replay:** Strategy Tester visual mode shows trades as Buy/Sell sign objects; Data Window; ticks capped at 64k in Market Watch during test [MT5, V]. (Speed/pause controls not described on that page.)
- **2026 releases:** builds 5800 (redesigned trading dialog with integrated DOM), 6060 (passkeys, MCP/AI assistant), 6140 (scale zoom indicators, axis click fixes), 6180/6230 (AI assistant adds indicators, reads EA inputs, economic calendar) [MT6, V]. Chart drawing moved to Blend2D in build 5430 (Nov 2025) [search excerpt, O].
- **Mobile:** "one tap" trading, built-in indicators/objects (no counts) [MT7, V].
- **Localization:** "up to 38 languages" incl. Arabic [MT8, C]; RTL mirroring not documented [?].
- **Standout:** P/L-in-currency tooltip while dragging SL/TP; DOM drag-across-spread flips order type.
- **Praise/complaints:** reliable, many timeframes/indicators, but UI "can feel dated", steeper learning curve [MT9, C].

### 1.5 cTrader (Spotware)

- **Chart types:** 8 — Bar, Candlestick, Line, Dot, Heikin Ashi, HLC, Renko, Range [CT2, O]; mobile chart-type menu: candle, line, bar, area, dot; Heikin Ashi/Renko/Range/Tick are period types [CT2, O].
- **Timeframes:** standard m1–M; **tick bars 1–1000 ticks per bar**; Renko and Range in pips [CT2, O]. Seconds bars: not found in official docs [?] (a YouTube plugin video sells custom seconds bars [C]).
- **Indicators:** 65–70+ built-in [CT5, C]; no official count found.
- **Chart trading:** QuickTrade Buy/Sell at top of chart (single or double click per settings); drag the QuickTrade box onto chart to place limit/stop (type decided by side of spread); drag order badge to change price, X to cancel; drag SL/TP from badge along price axis; **up to five take-profits** for scaling out; hovering a position badge shows Reverse, Double, Close [CT1, V].
- **Alerts:** right-click price → alert line (yellow), draggable, X to delete; email alerts [CT4, O].
- **Layouts:** Multi-chart, Single-chart, Free-chart (and Floating) modes; detached charts on Windows/Mac [CT3, O].
- **Shortcuts:** documented hotkey list (F2 chart mode, Ctrl+H hide objects, Alt+1/2 bar/candle, Ctrl+Shift+S Chartshot); customisable on Windows, not Web [CT4, O].
- **Chart Shots:** screenshot shared to browser link or saved to folder [CT4, O].
- **Standout:** multi-TP scale-out dragged directly from the chart; Reverse/Double/Close on hover; tick/Renko/Range as period types.
- **Praise/complaints:** modern, clean UI vs MT5 [MT9, C]; App Store complaint that a second short button used a different default lot size [CT6, C].

### 1.6 NinjaTrader 8 (desktop, futures-focused)

- **Bar types:** Tick, Volume, Range, Second/Minute/Day/Week/Month/Year, Heiken Ashi, Kagi, Renko, Point & Figure, Line Break (+ Order Flow volumetric types separately) [NT1, V].
- **Indicators:** "over 100 pre-built" [NT4, O]. Drawing tools ~30 [NT6, C].
- **Chart Trader:** resting orders as colour-coded lines with labels (LMT/STP/SLM/MIT); position line from entry label to a label showing size + **unrealized PnL (green/red)**, switchable among points/currency/pips/ticks/percent; "Show realized PnL when flat" [NT2, V; NT3, O]. Drag stop/target lines to modify (cancel/replace) [NT6, C]. ATM strategies = templated OCO stop/target brackets attached on entry [NT3, O].
- **Playback:** Market Replay up to 1,000× speed, slider to jump in time, 100 ns timestamps [NT5, O].
- **Standout:** configurable PnL unit on the position line; replay with sim trading.
- **Complaints:** recurring 2025–2026 forum reports of freezes/crashes, heavy resource use; praise for charting/DOM depth [NT7, C].

### 1.7 ChartIQ (S&P Global)

- **Ownership:** sold by Cosaic to **S&P Global** (signed 2022-12-23, closed early Jan 2023), part of S&P Global Market Intelligence; Finsemble spun off [CQ15, O]. Docs footer "Copyright © 2026 S&P Global" [CQ1, V]. Product pages now redirect from chartiq.com to spglobal.com.
- **Current version:** 10.x — 10.0.0 released 2025-10-20 (AI/CLI tools, Reference Data plugin, intelligent callouts with bar count/CAGR, Horizontal Line exact-price input, Table View keyboard nav, red-green colour-vision theme; legacy web components removed) [CQ2, V]; 10.1.0 made CLI plugin non-experimental [CQ search excerpt, O].
- **Studies:** **124** in the Built-in Studies Reference (TOC count) incl. Volatility Cone, Option Sentiment by Strike, Volume Profile, Anchored VWAP, Ichimoku, Supertrend, GoNoGo, Darvas, Pivot Points [CQ4, V].
- **Drawing tools:** **46** drawing classes (annotation, arrow, callout, channel, continuous, crossline, elliottwave, ellipse, fibarc/fibfan/fibprojection/fibtimezone/retracement, gannfan, gartley, measure/measurementline, pitchfork, quadrant, ray, rectangle, regression, segment, speedarc/speedline, timecycle, tirone, trendline, vertical, volumeprofile, freeform, longposition/shortposition, star/heart/check/xcross/focusarrow…) [CQ5, V]. Third-party "30+" [CQ16, C].
- **Chart styles/types:** Candle, Bar, Colored Bar, Line, Hollow Candle, Volume Candle, HLC, Mountain, Baseline, Step, Scatter; aggregations Heikin Ashi, Kagi, Line Break, Renko, Range Bars, Point & Figure [CQ8, O].
- **Magnet:** `preferences.magnet` (boolean/number) snaps drawing points to nearest OHLC [CQ9, O].
- **Studies on studies & comparisons:** any study with an `inputs.Field` can use another study's output or (since 9.0) a comparison series; `addSeries(..., {isComparison:true})` [CQ12, O].
- **Plugins/add-ons** (config): tfc (Trade From Chart), marketDepth (Active Trader), timeSpanEventPanel, crossSection (Term Structure), technicalInsights, technicalViews, signalIQ, studyBrowser, chart2music, cli, lowfrequency, ftsChartExplainer; add-ons tableView, rangeSlider, tooltip, shortcuts, dataLoader, extendedHours, fullScreen, inactivityTimer, animation, continuousZoom, forecasting, outliers; also hotkeys, themes (Day/Night + red-green friendly), language, chartSharing, multiChart, event markers [CQ6, V].
- **Trade From Chart (TFC):** quick market buy/sell widget; drag graphical widgets for limit/stop; Buy/Sell/Short/Cover with SL/TP protections; Spread, Straddle, Bracket widgets; OTO/OCO; modify open orders by dragging elements to new prices; `CIQ.Account` adapter for broker; poller syncs multiple TFC instances [CQ7, V].
- **Active Trader:** market depth chart + order book, scrolling tick chart, studies on sub-minute/sub-second bars, trade from order book [CQ13, O].
- **Options:** Options Analytics (since 8.1): IV/volume/OI/Greeks/bid-ask visualisation, Option Sentiment by Strike overlay, Volatility (Projection) Cone at 1σ/2σ from historical or implied vol (8.6, plugin in 8.8, `sample-template-options.html`), put/call ratios, IV history; `OptionsAnalysis` namespace [CQ10, O]. Term Structure of implied volatility via Cross Section plugin [CQ11, O]. No option-chain-overlay or 3D vol surface found [?].
- **Accessibility:** canvas keyboard-focusable and screen-reader readable (9.4), Table View, Chart2Music sonification integration (2025), colour-blind themes, command-prompt/conversational charting [CQ3, V; CQ14, O].
- **Performance claim:** "100,000+ data points", real-time to the millisecond [S&P use-case page via search, O/C].
- **Other:** Compact Chart (9.4, lightweight for dashboards/mobile), Hide All Drawings (Shift+Alt+H), pinned grouped markers [CQ3, V]; mobile SDK guidance for iOS/Android WebViews [CQ1, V].
- **Standout:** the richest *embeddable* options-analytics + accessibility package; TFC multi-leg widgets (straddle/spread) on chart.

### 1.8 SciChart (JS; native iOS/Android/WPF)

- **Performance claims (vendor):** WebAssembly + WebGL engine; 1M points at 60 FPS, 500 series at 60 FPS, append >1M points/s, up to "100 million data points at 60 FPS"; candlestick test claims millions of candles while competitors struggle at 1k–10k; native mobile 100k points at 60 FPS, 1M at 18 FPS [SC1, O/C — vendor marketing, not independently benchmarked].
- **Financial demos:** realtime ticking stock chart (Binance), multi-pane subcharts with MACD/RSI, user-annotated stock chart with serialisation, trading drawing tools package (polylines, channels, pitchforks, Fib retracement, measure, **stop-loss/take-profit regions**), hoverable buy/sell markers and news bullets, draggable event markers [SC2, O].
- **Flutter:** no official SDK; an experimental open-source wrapper over native iOS/Android (ABTSoftware/SciChart.Flutter) [SC3, O].
- **Standout:** raw throughput; relevant only as a performance benchmark/rendering-engine alternative, not as an end-user product.

## 5. Standout patterns worth copying

1. **Pull-out brackets from the position line** (TradingView): drag TP/SL out of the line; Shift-drag moves order+brackets [TV3]. Map to: drag barrier/strike out of a contract line on Deriv (4.1).
2. **Outcome tooltip while dragging** (MT5): potential P/L in currency and pips shown live [MT2]. Map to payout/probability preview while dragging a barrier (4.5).
3. **Hover actions on position badge** (cTrader Reverse/Double/Close) and **multi-TP scale-out** [CT1]. Map to "sell early", "duplicate contract", "close" on contract markers (3.7).
4. **PnL unit switch** (NT: currency/points/pips/ticks/%) [NT2]. Map to payout vs ROI% vs pips-to-barrier.
5. **Volatility/probability cone** projected into the future (ChartIQ 1σ/2σ) [CQ10]. Directly maps to Rise/Fall/Touch probability bands (5.3/5.4).
6. **T+0 vs expiry P&L curve** (TradingView Strategy Builder) [TV6] → payoff diagram for Vanillas/Turbos (5.1).
7. **Expiring price alerts** (LWC plugin) [LWC4] → expiry countdown/expiring markers (3.5).
8. **Options chart with price on x-axis** (LWC 5.0) [LWC3] → strike ladder / payout-by-barrier view (5.7).
9. **Replay with trading** (TV Bar Replay incl. brackets; NT Playback 1000×) [TV1,TV4,NT5] → contract replay (7.1).
10. **Accessibility stack** (ChartIQ table view + Chart2Music sonification; TVL screen-reader support) [CQ14,TVL4].
11. **Throughput budget** (SciChart: 1M pts @60 FPS claim; LWC data conflation) [SC1,LWC2] → set explicit tick-rate/fps targets for Flutter renderer (11.1).

## 6. Limitations

- No product was used hands-on; no login/demo accounts. Counts are documentation/marketing figures.
- spglobal.com and scichart.com returned HTTP 403 to fetch; their facts come from search excerpts (O) and are vendor claims.
- TradingView pricing-table extraction lost some cells; per-plan mappings for replay history are inferred.
- TradingView options "win rate"/probability metric is third-party only; not confirmed in TV docs.
- cTrader indicator count and drawing-tool list are third-party; seconds bars in cTrader not confirmed either way.
- NinjaTrader drag-to-modify and drawing-tool count are third-party.
- ChartIQ study count is from the reference-guide TOC (124); drawing count is class count (46) and includes decorative shapes (star/heart/check).
- MT5 localisation count and RTL behaviour unverified.
- "Praise/complaints" are anecdotal (Trustpilot, forums, broker blogs); no systematic Reddit sampling succeeded (searches returned no matching threads).
- Accessibility and performance for TV app, MT5, cTrader, NT left "?" — no official statements found within time budget.
